Coordinates and offline maps

Every station carries a latitude and longitude so a map link works with no signal, provided the Steamboat area is downloaded to the phone first.

Verify these before race week. The coordinates here are approximate, taken from place locations rather than surveyed. Check them against the official CalTopo course map, and never navigate a vehicle to Summit Lake or Fish Creek Falls with a map app.

In Google Maps: search Steamboat Springs, tap the name, then Download offline map. Do it on wifi before you leave.
