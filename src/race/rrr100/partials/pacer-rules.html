The hard rules

  • One pacer at a time. The only exception is the last four miles from the top of the gondola.
  • You may only get on or off at five places: Dry Lake 44.5, Olympian Hall 51.2, Olympian Hall 63.9, Dry Lake 70.8, Summit Lake 80.8.
  • No headphones between Spring Creek Trailhead and Olympian Hall, either direction.
  • Check in at every aid station, including both passes through Lane of Pain.
  • Get yourself to and from the stop. Vehicles and non-pacer crew are not allowed at non-crew stations.
