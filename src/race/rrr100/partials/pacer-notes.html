Muling

The race directors are relaxed about this for Tortoises. Handing him a torch or a bar, or holding his bottle while he is sick, is fine. Carrying his pack for miles is not. You are there for safety, company, and keeping him on course.

What to bring for yourself

Nobody is crewing you. Bring your own headlamp and a spare, warm layers, a rain shell, gloves, water and all your own food. Aid stations will feed you but do not count on it, and Billy's Rabbit Hole and Lane of Pain are minimal.

Expect four to six hours out. It drops below freezing above 10,000 feet at night.

Keeping him moving

Your job at every stop is to get him out of it. Ten minutes, not thirty. Olympian Hall is heated and indoors and it ends more races than the climbs do.

Make him eat and drink on a schedule, out loud, whether he wants to or not. By mile seventy he will not be making good decisions and you will be making them for him.
