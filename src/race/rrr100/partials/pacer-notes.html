Muling

The race directors are relaxed about this for Tortoises. Handing over a torch or a bar, or holding a bottle while your runner is sick, is fine. Carrying the pack for miles is not. You are there for safety, company, and keeping your runner on course.

What to bring for yourself

Nobody is crewing you. Bring your own headlamp and a spare, warm layers, a rain shell, gloves, water and all your own food. Aid stations will feed you but do not count on it, and Billy's Rabbit Hole and Lane of Pain are minimal.

Expect four to six hours out. It drops below freezing above 10,000 feet at night.

Keeping them moving

Your job at every stop is to get your runner out of it. Ten minutes, not thirty. Olympian Hall is heated and indoors and it ends more races than the climbs do.

Make them eat and drink on a schedule, out loud, whether they want to or not. By mile seventy your runner will not be making good decisions and you will be making them.
