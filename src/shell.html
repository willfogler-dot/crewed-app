Good to know

The rules that matter, and who's who.

Three ways to get Will disqualified

1. Never drive to Fish Creek Falls Trailhead. You may walk or bike there — four miles from Olympian Hall — but a car in that lot can end his race.

2. Never drive up Buffalo Pass Road past Dry Lake. Not once, not for a second. Summit Lake is the long way round, about two hours.

3. Never park at Dry Lake. Pull in, let people out, turn round and leave. The sheriff tickets and tows.

Handing him things

You may only help him at the stops listed in this app, plus anywhere in town between Spring Creek Trailhead and Olympian Hall. Nowhere else. If you are not sure, don't — ask a volunteer first.

Be kind to the volunteers. The manual says plainly that being rude to them is grounds for disqualification, and they are all unpaid.

If you can't find him

There is tracking by bib number on the race website, though much of the course has no signal so it lags. Do not phone the race asking where he is. They know less than you do. If he is more than about two hours past the time shown here, tell a volunteer at the next stop and they will radio ahead.

Pacer rules

Get these wrong and it is Will's race that ends, not yours.

The hard rules

  • One pacer at a time. The only exception is the last four miles from the top of the gondola.
  • You may only get on or off at five places: Dry Lake 44.5, Olympian Hall 51.2, Olympian Hall 63.9, Dry Lake 70.8, Summit Lake 80.8.
  • No headphones between Spring Creek Trailhead and Olympian Hall, either direction.
  • Check in at every aid station, including both passes through Lane of Pain.
  • Get yourself to and from the stop. Vehicles and non-pacer crew are not allowed at non-crew stations.

Muling

The race directors are relaxed about this for Tortoises. Handing him a torch or a bar, or holding his bottle while he is sick, is fine. Carrying his pack for miles is not. You are there for safety, company, and keeping him on course.

What to bring for yourself

Nobody is crewing you. Bring your own headlamp and a spare, warm layers, a rain shell, gloves, water and all your own food. Aid stations will feed you but do not count on it, and Billy's Rabbit Hole and Lane of Pain are minimal.

Expect four to six hours out. It drops below freezing above 10,000 feet at night.

Keeping him moving

Your job at every stop is to get him out of it. Ten minutes, not thirty. Olympian Hall is heated and indoors and it ends more races than the climbs do.

Make him eat and drink on a schedule, out loud, whether he wants to or not. By mile seventy he will not be making good decisions and you will be making them for him.

Coordinates and offline maps

Every station carries a latitude and longitude so a map link works with no signal, provided the Steamboat area is downloaded to the phone first.

Verify these before race week. The coordinates here are approximate, taken from place locations rather than surveyed. Check them against the official CalTopo course map, and never navigate a vehicle to Summit Lake or Fish Creek Falls with a map app.

In Google Maps: search Steamboat Springs, tap the name, then Download offline map. Do it on wifi before you leave.
